## Sound collection

The complete pack contains 21 recordings supplied by the project owner and
seven procedurally synthesized sounds. The owner requested publication of the
supplied recordings and stated that they are royalty-free and require no license.
The exact statement, provenance limitations and original file hashes are recorded
in sounds/PROVENANCE.json and sounds/RIGHTS.txt. No CC0 or GPL grant is made for
those recordings by FartMC.

The seven sounds generated by scripts/SynthesizeSounds.java use mathematical
oscillators and seeded noise. Those generated sounds are dedicated under CC0-1.0;
see licenses/CC0-1.0.txt. The generator code remains GPL-3.0-only.

Historical implementation provenance is documented in docs/REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md.
No external implementation binary, decompiled code or unapproved audio is bundled.

## Food models

Food models and textures supplied for the 2.3.0 update are recorded separately in
